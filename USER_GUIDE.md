# ICAR Sample Registration — User Guide

This app registers a new ICAR sample (or logs an action on an existing one)
and gives you a unique sample ID plus a printable label with a QR code.

## Before you start

Have on hand:
- A brief description of the sample (10 words or fewer)
- Your name, email, and ICAR institution
- Where the sample came from (source institution)
- A link to the sample's existing record, if it has one already (IGSN
  resolver link, catalog page, etc.) — otherwise leave blank
- Where the sample physically is right now

## Steps

1. **Open the app** at [app URL — ask your admin if you don't have it].

2. **Pick the sample's broad type first**, at the top of the page:
   `Organism`, `Rock`, `Blob`, `Ice`, `Mixed`, or `Extract`.
   Depending on what you pick, extra questions will appear right below it:
   - **Organism** → pick Multicellular, Community, or Microbe
   - **Rock** → pick Primitive/Igneous/Metaphoric/Sedimentary, then answer
     Organic? and Amorphous?
   - **Blob** → answer AqSoluble (water soluble)? and Macromolecular?
   - **Ice** → answer Water ice? and pick Solid or Liquid
   - **Mixed** and **Extract** have no extra questions

3. **Fill in the form below that:**
   - **Brief description** — 10 words or fewer. If you go over, the app
     will tell you to shorten it.
   - **Who is registering it** — your name, email, and ICAR institution
     (pick from the dropdown).
   - **Sample provenance** — the source institution (e.g. ATCC, a museum),
     an existing sample URL if there is one, and the sample's current
     physical location.
   - **Action & notes** — what you're doing right now (registering a new
     sample, running an analysis, altering/processing it, or something
     else — pick "Other" and describe it). Notes is optional — use it for
     hazards or links to protocols.

4. **Click "Register Sample."**
   You'll get:
   - A unique sample ID (three words, like `eager-bullmastiff-of-tact`)
   - A printable label with that ID, a QR code, and blank spots for
     status/mass to fill in by hand
   - A **Download label PNG** button — download and print it, then attach
     it to the physical sample

## Troubleshooting

- **"Please fill in: ..."** — one or more required fields (marked `*`)
  were left blank; the message tells you which.
- **"Brief description must be 10 words or fewer"** — shorten it and
  resubmit; nothing was saved yet.
- **Something else looks wrong or the app errors out** — contact
  sunanda@exsitu.bio with what you were doing when it happened.
